| `04-strings` | Strings | `01`–`04` | 20–23 |

The exercises come from [Java Exercises – GeeksforGeeks](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/java/java-exercises/); the problem statements are on the original page. Exercises are numbered per module; the *GfG #* column gives the original numbering.
